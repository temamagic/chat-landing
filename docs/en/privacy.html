  • USE OF COOKIES
  • To improve the Service and make it more user-friendly, special cookie technology may be used, which, in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation, may be considered Personal Data. In this section, you can learn what cookies are and how the Service Owner uses them.
  • What are cookies?
  • Cookies are small data files that the Service stores on your computer and uses to record certain actions on the Service by storing data that can be updated and retrieved. They can be used to record your preferences, such as selected language and login details, as well as for personalizing the Service.
  • Cookies may also be used to store information about how you use the Service. For example, which page or which advertising "banner" led you to the site.
  • What types of cookies are used on the Service and for what purposes?
  • Session cookies: temporary cookies stored in your browser’s cookies until you leave the page; these files are not stored on your hard drive. The information collected through these files helps the Service Owner analyze network traffic and improve the content and usability of the Service.
  • Persistent cookies: these files are stored on your computer’s hard drive, and the Service recognizes them each time you visit. Persistent cookies have an expiration date. After this date, they stop functioning. Persistent cookies allow the Service to remember information and settings for future visits, making browsing more convenient and faster, for example, by eliminating the need to re-enter registration data.
  • Technical cookies: technical cookies are necessary for the Service Owner to ensure the proper functioning of the Service and the correct use of various personal features and services offered on it. For example, these cookies can be used for session support, response time control, confirming personal preferences, using security elements, data transfer through social networks, etc.
  • Personalization cookies: these cookies allow you to choose or personalize certain features of the Service, such as setting the language, regional configuration, or browser type.
  • Analytics cookies: the Service uses cookies to collect information about browser settings and analyze your behavior on the site to improve performance and help select products and services that interest you. In particular, using analytics cookies, the Service Owner can gather information about the products that are most suitable for you, etc.
  • Your options for managing cookies
  • The Service Owner will ask for your separate consent to process cookies. If you do not provide such consent, the Service Owner will only process technical cookies that are necessary for the proper functioning of the Service.
  • You can also withdraw your consent to process cookies at any time and delete cookies stored on your computer by changing your browser settings.
  • Finally, if you want to control the cookie installation process, you can install special programs or add-ons in your browser known as tracking prevention tools, allowing you to choose which cookies you allow to be stored on your computer.
