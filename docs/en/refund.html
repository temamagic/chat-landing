Return Policy

Updated: November 26, 2024

  • This Return Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") regulates the procedure for returning funds for the use of the paid functionality of the Liza Virtual Assistant service (hereinafter referred to as the "Service").

1. General Provisions

  • 1.1. The Return Policy is an integral part of the User Agreement.
  • 1.2. By using the paid services of the Service, you agree to the terms of this Policy.
  • 1.3. This Policy applies to all paid subscriptions and services provided through the Service.

2. Conditions for Returns

  • 2.1. A refund will be made in the following cases:
  • Double charging for the same service.
  • Technical errors that prevent the use of the paid service due to the Operator's fault.
  • Services provided with significant deviations from the stated specifications.
  • 2.2. No refund will be made in the following cases:
  • Partial use of the service or subscription.
  • User dissatisfaction with the Service's functionality in the absence of technical errors.
  • Expiration of the subscription period.

3. Procedure for Requesting a Refund

  • 3.1. To request a refund, the User must contact our support service:
  • Email: info@liza.bot
  • 3.2. The request must include:
  • User's name.
  • Details about the service or subscription (payment date, amount).
  • The reason for the refund request.
  • If necessary — screenshots or other supporting materials.
  • 3.3. Refund requests will be processed within 14 (fourteen) calendar days from the receipt of the complete information package.

4. Refund Methods

  • 4.1. Refunds will be made using the same payment method that was used to pay for the service.
  • 4.2. The time it takes for the funds to be credited to the User's account depends on the bank or payment system's terms and may take up to 30 calendar days.

5. Changes to the Policy

  • 5.1. The Operator reserves the right to change this Policy. The current version will be published on the website https://liza.bot.
  • 5.2. Changes take effect upon their publication.
